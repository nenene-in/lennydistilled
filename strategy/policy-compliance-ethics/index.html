Common Questions

How can I ensure my product's content moderation policies are fair and consistent?

Embrace transparency by making your content policies, data, and algorithms publicly available. This allows users to understand how decisions are made and hold the platform accountable. Resist the temptation to make exceptions or manipulate the system - apply the same standards evenly, even for high-profile figures or advertisers.

How can I ensure the AI systems I develop are secure and trustworthy?

AI security is an ongoing challenge that requires continuous vigilance. No matter how thoroughly you test your AI models, new vulnerabilities can always emerge. The best approach is to adopt an "AI red teaming" mindset, where you proactively try to find ways the AI could be misused or behave in unexpected ways. This helps you stay one step ahead of potential threats.

Do I really need to dive deep into all the details and regulations in my industry? Isn't it enough to have a high-level understanding?

In highly regulated industries, a deep understanding of the details and nuances of policies and regulations is essential. Overlooking or mishandling these critical elements can have serious consequences, even if your product or service is otherwise successful. As a product manager, you need to master the complexities of your industry to keep both your customers and regulators satisfied.

How do I ensure the software I manage aligns with our company's values and principles?

Open source software gives users the freedom to inspect, modify, and distribute the code, ensuring it remains transparent and aligned with your organization's ethical standards. Embracing open source principles in your product management can help you build software that empowers users and maintains your company's integrity.

See what experts say
How do I balance prioritizing my company's profits and growth with serving our customers' interests?

It's important to keep your mission and the greater good at the forefront, even when it comes at the expense of short-term profits. Focus on maximizing human flourishing, not just margins. Making decisions that prioritize your customers' needs and the public good will pay off in the long run through stronger customer loyalty and brand reputation.
