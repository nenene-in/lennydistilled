AI changes everything about moats

Strategy → Market Positioning

Defining

Based on the transcript context, "mode" appears to be used incorrectly and should likely be "moat" - referring to competitive advantages that protect businesses from competitors.

You want to build something that the most general model will not replace you. And oftentimes you build something and you make it really, really good for specific set of users and actually the mode is now in your user feedback. The mode is in whether you listen to them, whether you can rapidly iterate.
