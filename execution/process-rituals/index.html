Common Questions

How will AI change the way my team works on a daily basis? I'm worried it will disrupt our existing processes and collaboration.

AI is actually changing work patterns in a way that can make your team more productive. Rather than disrupting collaboration, AI can help your team get into flow faster and maintain focus during deep work sessions. By automating certain tasks, AI frees up your team to be more creative and strategic.

How can I balance the need for speed in delivery with the need for learning and discovery in my product development process?

Instead of just focusing on fast delivery, build in opportunities to learn and adapt throughout your process. Treat each step as a learning milestone - build something, see what you discover, and let that guide your next moves. This allows you to find the right balance between speed and discovery, so you can deliver a better product more efficiently.

How can I ensure that my product team makes decisions quickly and effectively?

Clearly assign decision-making authority to one person rather than trying to reach consensus. This "decider" should be accountable for the results, enabling faster execution. Once they make a call, the rest of the team should commit to supporting it, rather than escalating or reopening the decision.

How much process and structure should I introduce as my product management team scales?

Introducing too much process can stifle innovation and creativity within your team. While some structure is necessary for consistency, focus on lightweight, flexible processes that preserve your team's ability to experiment and respond quickly to changing needs. Striking the right balance will help your team maintain high performance as you grow.

How can I ensure my team is making good use of their time and not getting bogged down in endless planning?

Rather than focusing on making perfect estimates, set a reasonable time "appetite" for each feature or project. This forces the team to prioritize and make tough decisions to fit within the allotted time, leading to more focused and impactful work. Avoid the tendency for work to expand to fill all available time.
