Platform teams need different validation cycles

Execution → Working with Engineering

As a platform PM, your psychology has to really change around validation. The cycles are five to ten times longer. You're not designing the end user's experience - you're designing a canvas for developers to build their own creative ideas on.

Brandon Chu Lessons from scaling Uber and Opendoor | Brian Tolkin (Head of Product at Opendoor, ex-Uber)
Supporting

Why don't you figure out what the scalable solution is for this? We will build you a product. You won't need to come ask us every time to build something. And then you'll know the parameters in which you can negotiate.

Keith Yandell Leading with empathy | Keith Yandell (DoorDash, Uber)
Supporting

The really radical thing Palantir said was, 'No. Go in and if you need a completely new product to do this, you can go ahead and build it.'

Nabeel S. Qureshi How Palantir built the ultimate founder factory

Ryan J. Salva The role of AI in new product development
