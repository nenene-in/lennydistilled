Lessons from scaling Stripe

Featuring: Claire Hughes Johnson (Former COO of Stripe)

12 quotes · 11 insights

Watch Full Episode

Organizations run on energy that dissipates from the center

Don't make somebody the CMO, or even the head of marketing, if your marketing team is two people... You want more flexibility.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:16:43

Leaders create leverage by developing others

Your job as the manager is to enable people to be their very damn best on your team.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 38:05

Pivot toward expertise, not away from pain

Product market fit is just the product, and that is not a company, and that will not scale.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:20:23

Own problems beyond your scope to create momentum

Be a force for positive momentum and it will be actually a real career maker.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:00:00

Politics and interests complicate pure strategy

Having just the right amount of tension in our relationship was how you knew it was working for the company.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 01:08:11

Address small conflicts before they become catastrophic

Ask a question. Make an observation that you own. So if I said, 'I feel like there's something we're not talking about, and I wonder...' Oh, I own it.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 36:32

Overcommunicate your vision - repetition is necessary

Never think that one communication, meaning an email or an all hands, reaches the audience. You have to be smart about how you communicate.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 01:12:02

Success comes from deciding, not deliberating

If you're not sure who the decision maker is, one, it's probably you. And I'd rather you act that way than not because you're going to like slow the whole company down.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:00:00

Explicit clarity prevents wasted cycles

Make the thing that's implicit explicit.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 01:16:16

External wisdom beats internal assumptions

Anytime we were confronted with anything, even things I felt I'd done before, that I had the experience and we were going to build it, they were like, 'Well, who have you talked to?' Or 'What have you read?'
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:10:21

Build systems, not one-offs

Don't let the perfect be the enemy of the good.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:50:35
Do very few things consistently and try to do them well, and then see if they're working for you.
Claire Hughes JohnsonFormer COO of Stripe 00:49:16
